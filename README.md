# WISE AI Dojo

A free, phone-friendly AI literacy course for women entrepreneurs (Pinaypreneurs), made for the
**Women in Sustainable Enterprises (WISE) Program: Enterprise Development, Business Continuity and
Resilience Planning for Pinaypreneurs**, Kabankalan City, Negros Occidental, Philippines, October 27 – 29, 2026.

**Open the Dojo:** https://akiramurata-juntendo-code.github.io/wise-ai-dojo/

## What is inside

| Tab | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| My Dojo | A 5-minute AI Match Quiz gives each learner an AI level (1 to 5) and her own path of practice prompts, using free AI apps on a phone. |
| Lead your day | Day 1 morning lesson: 5 short lecture videos, a tap-to-colour 24-hour clock, AI prompts and a one-page PDF of "My 24 hours". |
| Sell smarter | Day 1 afternoon and Day 2 course: customers, promise, Facebook and Messenger, records, disaster days and a 30-day plan, with 6 AI tasks. |
| Handouts | The lecture slides as PDF. |
| Facilitator guide | How to run the Dojo with about 30 participants. |

## Privacy

- Nothing is collected by this website. Answers are kept only in the learner's own browser (localStorage).
  "Clear all my answers on this phone" removes them, for shared phones.
- "Send my answers" is optional: it opens a Google Form already filled in, and the learner chooses whether to submit.
  Research use only after ethics approval.
- Learners are reminded never to type an MPIN, OTP, password, or customers' names, phone numbers or addresses into AI.

## Files

```
index.html     the whole Dojo (one page, no build step)
video/         lecture videos, Lead your day Parts 1–5 (MP4, 720p) and poster images
handouts/      slide PDFs and cover images
qr/            QR code and printable A4 poster
og.jpg         preview image for links shared on Facebook and Messenger
.nojekyll      tells GitHub Pages to serve the files as they are
```

To update the site, replace `index.html` (or a file in `video/` or `handouts/`) and push to `main`.
GitHub Pages publishes the change within a few minutes.
